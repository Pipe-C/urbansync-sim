# Landing `/` — surface brief

Scope: one public Persuade surface, the first page at `/`. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: a professor/evaluator or student arriving cold. Job: understand what
UrbanSync Sim is and why adaptive control matters, then act. Actions: two doors —
**Entrar** (`/login`, operator/demo) and **Observar** (`/observer`, no credentials).
Proof/content: the product's unique mechanism — a paired adaptativo-vs-fijo
comparison over the same vehicle sequence, with an explainable, rule-based law.
No fabricated metrics: the landing demonstrates the mechanism live; measured
numbers live in `/dashboard`. Constraints: Control Room world, Spanish UI,
`aria-hidden` scene, no business claims. Anti-goals: headline-over-subcopy-and-
button, a row of equal cards, a painted poster with no working parts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The intersection is the central monitor of a control desk, and the
product's claims are the instruments wired around it. It refuses the category
default of a headline, subcopy and a button over a row of equal cards, and it
refuses to make the scene a backdrop — the scene is the subject the instruments
read.

OWN-WORLD: The Control Room, inherited verbatim: Console Black ground, Surface
Navy panels, teal as the only interactive voice, the four signal colours reserved
for traffic state, Inter for prose and JetBrains Mono for every machine value,
1px hairlines, flat-by-default depth. Added, on loan from the VU bridge: meter
faces with inked scales and one needle per claim that eases with real ballistics,
the arc past the mark the single place a signal colour may appear without a live
traffic state behind it.

STORY: The visitor learns it is a live adaptive intersection, that its whole
argument is a paired comparison on one vehicle sequence with an explainable law,
and that they can run the console or simply watch. They believe the control is
real because it is already running before they touch anything.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-height desk. A top rule names UrbanSync Sim and its one
line. Centre, the live ambient intersection seated in a screen bezel, with a mono
caption naming the two streets. Beside it, a short stack of instrument faces —
the paired comparison, and preemption — each a labelled meter. Under the desk, the
action row: **Entrar** (primary, teal) and **Observar** (secondary). No eyebrow or
kicker; the h1 is the product name itself.

FORM: instrument rack, position 6 of the ordered grounded list, seed key
`eb15258e`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
